# PRODUCT.md — SHM (Support Hours Manager)

## Product Mission
SHM is a B2B SaaS platform engineered to provide total transparency, financial clarity, and operational rigor for software support contracts. It transforms nebulous client requests into structured, client-approved execution cycles with real-time tracking of contracted, inherited, and consumed hours.

## Target Personas
1. **Client Manager (`GESTOR_CLIENTE`)**: Needs instant executive certainty regarding contract health, hours available, pending cycle budgets, and completed deliverables before granting final acceptance.
2. **Client Standard User (`USUARIO_CLIENTE`)**: Needs an effortless, friction-free way to log support requests and follow up via conversation timeline.
3. **Provider Administrator (`ADMIN_EMPRESA`)**: Requires comprehensive visibility across all active client contracts, rollover windows (30-day default with manual extension), and unbilled negative balances for supplemental invoicing.
4. **Provider Support Manager (`GESTOR_SUPORTE`)**: Needs tools to rapidly triage incoming requests, split them into distinct maintenance cycles, estimate hours, and assign technical tasks.
5. **Technical Specialist (`TECNICO`)**: Needs a focused, distraction-free view of assigned tasks with rapid inline logging of actual hours executed.

## Primary Mode
- **Operate**: The interface is an operational command center. Scanability, contrast, high-speed task completion, and numeric accuracy always take precedence over decorative flair.

## Key Product Truths & Principles
- **Hours are Financial Assets**: Every decimal of an hour represents real monetary value. Numbers must never be obscured, rounded ambiguously, or hidden.
- **Deductions Only on Client Acceptance**: Hours are deducted from active balances *only* when the Client Manager explicitly confirms final acceptance.
- **Negative Balance is a Feature**: The system never halts critical client support due to zero balance; negative balances are highlighted with high visual urgency for subsequent rollover or invoicing.
- **Zero Ambiguity in Actions**: Action buttons must state their explicit consequence (e.g., *"Confirmar Aceite Final (Deduzir 8.50h)"* instead of *"Confirmar"*).
